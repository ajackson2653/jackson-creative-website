Services

Surveys that turn questions into answers.

We offer a variety of survey instruments to meet your needs. Each one is designed, fielded, and analyzed to give you insight you can actually act on.

01 — Employee Engagement Surveys

Understand how your team really feels.

Conduct regular surveys of your employees to gauge both their satisfaction with their job and their engagement with the organization. The result is a clear picture of where you stand — and where to focus.

  • Satisfaction & engagement measurement
  • Benchmarking over time
  • Confidential data collection
  • Clear, actionable reporting
02 — Customer Experience Surveys

Will they return — and recommend you?

Survey your customers to understand their willingness to return or to recommend you to others. We help you find the moments that build loyalty and the friction that costs you business.

  • Satisfaction & loyalty (NPS-style) metrics
  • Willingness to return & refer
  • Online, written, or phone-based fielding
  • Insight tied to specific touchpoints
03 — Community Awareness Surveys

Measure how you're perceived.

Conduct surveys to understand perceptions and awareness of your organization, product, or service — and to predict interest in upcoming events or changes before you commit to them.

  • Brand & organizational awareness
  • Perception & reputation measurement
  • Interest in new events or initiatives
  • Representative community sampling
04 — Legal Case Awareness Surveys

Insight for a fair and unbiased trial.

A very specific form of community surveying designed to gauge awareness of a legal case — helping counsel understand whether a change of venue should be considered to ensure a fair, unbiased trial.

  • Pre-trial publicity & awareness measurement
  • Change-of-venue support
  • Rigorous, defensible methodology
  • Clear expert-ready reporting
