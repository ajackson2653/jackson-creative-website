Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Jackson Insight ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting information through our forms, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us — such as your name, email address, mobile phone number, and any details you share when you contact us, request services, or sign up to receive text messages. We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type and pages visited, to operate and improve our site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, provide and improve our survey research services, communicate with you about projects, and send the messages you have requested. We do not sell your personal information.

Text Messaging (SMS)

Text Messaging (SMS): No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers collected for text messaging are used solely to send the survey invitations and reminders you have consented to receive. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

If you opt in to receive text messages, you can withdraw your consent at any time by replying STOP to any message. For assistance, reply HELP. Opting out of text messages will not affect any other communications you have chosen to receive from us.

How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (for example, hosting or survey-delivery platforms), only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services and under obligations of confidentiality. We may also disclose information if required by law. As stated above, mobile information collected for text messaging is never sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP, and you may contact us to request access to or deletion of your information. To exercise these choices, use the contact details below.
